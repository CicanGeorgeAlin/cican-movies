# CICAN MOVIES — Rejected / Held Research Ledger

This is an internal research ledger, not part of the public movie catalogue.

A candidate placed here has been investigated but did not pass the current CICAN publication gate. **Rejected/held does not mean permanently rejected.** The title may become catalogue-ready if new rights evidence, an authorized playback source, a complete feature file, subtitle evidence, corrected identity data, or other missing evidence is found later.

## Rules

- Never expose this ledger as public catalogue content.
- Never treat a ledger entry as playable.
- Never count ledger entries in the public movie counter.
- Preserve the original investigation evidence and the reason for the hold/rejection.
- Re-investigate candidates when new sources, rights evidence, restorations, licences, or playback routes become available.
- Do not delete a candidate merely because the current investigation failed.
- A later investigation may change the status to READY_TO_IMPORT if all publication gates pass.
- Keep rights conclusions jurisdiction-specific; do not turn a single-country public-domain finding into a worldwide claim.
- Do not use piracy, unauthorized mirrors, torrents, DRM/access-control circumvention, or other illegitimate acquisition routes.

## Status vocabulary

- `HELD` — potentially viable, but one or more important checks remain unresolved.
- `REJECTED_CURRENTLY` — investigated and currently fails a publication requirement.
- `RECHECK` — intentionally queued for future investigation.
- `PROMOTED` — later passed investigation and was moved into the public catalogue.

## Record format

Each candidate should record, where known:

- Title
- Year
- Original language
- Country / production origin
- Investigation date
- Discovery source(s)
- Playback source(s) investigated
- Feature-length verification
- Rights status and jurisdiction
- Subtitle/language findings
- Identity / duplicate findings
- Exact reason for hold/rejection
- Missing evidence or next investigation target
- Last checked
- Recheck priority
- Later outcome / promotion commit
